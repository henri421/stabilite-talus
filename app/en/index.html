Slope stability

General stability of a slope, cut or embankment by the simplified Bishop method, in 1st or 2nd generation.

Not covered: non-circular surfaces, inclined layers, reinforcements and nails, seismic action, tension cracks, suction, free water above the ground, serviceability limit states.

Standard slope…

“Standard slope” fills the profile from the height, the slope and the crest and toe lengths: the usual case without a table of points. The x values must increase.

Layers (horizontal boundaries)

A layer runs from its base up to the layer above, or to the ground. “Undrained” replaces φ’k and c’k by cu,k. Both behaviours can coexist from one layer to the next.

Circle

Empty, no limit. The depth limit stands for a stiff stratum that the circle does not cross.

“Search for the critical” looks for the most unfavourable circle for each combination: the circle of DA1-1 is not necessarily that of DA1-2.

Generation and factors

With ANB:2022, the approach is fixed to DA1 (greyed list), and the risk (low, normal, high) is taken into account.

The 2nd generation offers only the consequence class: VC3 + M2, material approach (EN 1997-3, table 4.2).

Switching generation keeps the data.

Out of scope

Not covered: non-circular surfaces, inclined layers, reinforcements and nails, seismic action, tension cracks, suction, free water above the ground, serviceability limit states.
